Privacy Policy for Dino Run

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

Welcome to Dino Run. We are committed to protecting your privacy while you enjoy playing our app. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information that we receive when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Dino Run is designed as a privacy-conscious mobile game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

2. Local Game Data

All gameplay statistics, high scores, unlocked biomes, player progress, and audio settings are saved locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers or shared with any external parties.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The app may interact with essential framework services provided by Google Play Store (such as Google Play Games Services for achievements or leaderboard support, if enabled). These services operate under Google's own Privacy Policy.

We do not track your location, access your contacts, or monitor your activity outside of the application.

4. Children's Privacy

Our game is designed to be family-friendly and suitable for players of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information from any users, including children under the age of 13, Dino Run complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

5. Device Permissions

Dino Run requires minimal device permissions to function correctly (such as network access for store validation or storage access to save local settings). We do not request access to sensitive permissions such as your camera, microphone, precise location, or contact list.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
